# SYNONYM Design Guidelines

Read \`docs/brand/SYNONYM-DESIGN-GUIDELINES.md\` and actual brand source. The Missoma reference informs structural UX only; do not copy its visual assets or promotional claims.

Maintain existing 6-block homepage and the original four image category cards unchanged. Use Playfair Display headings, Inter body and generous readable spacing. Treat bright green and terracotta as accents, dark olive as high-contrast buttons, sand/warm white as supporting backgrounds.

Product photography, SKU, stock, variant prices **only from AdvantShop**. Editorial campaign photos and videos come from GitHub via \`src/config/synonym-media.json\`. No fabricated influencer reviews, inventory, discounts, policies or gemstones.

Apply responsive tests at 360/390/768/820/1024/1440/1920. Keep keyboard focus, contrast and reduced-motion behavior. Do not modify production or checkout as part of visual refinements.
